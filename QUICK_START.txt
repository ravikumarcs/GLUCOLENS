WHAT IS GLUCOLENS?
================================================================================

GlucoLens analyzes Glooko diabetes data and generates personalized Omnipod 
insulin pump setting recommendations including:
  • Basal rate profiles (hourly insulin delivery rates)
  • Insulin sensitivity factors (correction factors)
  • Carb-to-insulin ratios
  • Target glucose ranges
  • Safety limits (max bolus, max basal)

DATA FORMAT SUPPORTED
================================================================================

CSV FORMAT:
  - Column: timestamp (e.g., "2024-01-15 08:00:00")
  - Column: glucose_value (mg/dL)
  - Column: carbs (grams, optional)
  - Column: insulin_amount (units, optional)

JSON FORMAT:
  {
    "glucose_readings": [...],
    "meals": [...],
    "insulin_events": [...]
  }

See docs/GETTING_STARTED.md for detailed examples.

================================================================================
WHAT YOU GET
================================================================================

For each hour of the day (0-23), you receive:

BASAL RATES:
  6:00 AM  → 0.55 units/hour (higher due to dawn phenomenon)
  Noon     → 0.50 units/hour (standard)
  Evening  → 0.53 units/hour (adjusted for circadian rhythm)

INSULIN SENSITIVITY FACTOR (ISF):
  6:00 AM  → 1:85 (1 unit covers 85 mg/dL - more resistant)
  Noon     → 1:100 (1 unit covers 100 mg/dL - standard)
  10 PM    → 1:110 (1 unit covers 110 mg/dL - more sensitive)

CARB RATIOS:
  7:00 AM  → 1 unit per 12.7g carbs (morning resistance)
  Noon     → 1 unit per 15g carbs (standard)
  6:00 PM  → 1 unit per 16.5g carbs (more sensitive)

PLUS:
  • Time in range analysis (% time 70-180 mg/dL)
  • Glucose variability metrics
  • Low glucose event detection
  • Meal response patterns
  • Target glucose recommendations

================================================================================
COMMON PATTERNS EXPLAINED
================================================================================

DAWN PHENOMENON:
  - Higher glucose needs in early morning (4-8 AM)
  - You'll see higher basal rates and lower ISF (more resistant)
  - This is normal and reflects your body's circadian rhythm

INSULIN RESISTANCE BY TIME OF DAY:
  - Morning: Most resistant (need more insulin)
  - Afternoon: Moderate resistance
  - Evening: Most sensitive (need less insulin)

MEAL RESPONSE:
  - Peak glucose typically 1-2 hours after eating
  - Used to calibrate carb ratios
  - Higher carb meals = larger glucose rise
